# EASE-Delta: pre-registered evaluation plan

Written 2026-09-29, before any test split was evaluated. At the time of writing the Stage A edge
model was still training and no downstream component had been trained. The SHA-256 of this file is
recorded in `runs/prereg_hash.txt`; `docs/RESULTS.md` repeats it. Anything done differently from
this plan is listed under "Deviations" in the results, with the reason.

## 1. What is being tested

The claim under test is architectural, and narrow:

> A decision system that reads each (claim, record) pair separately, caches those readings, and
> combines them through an exactly executed policy can (a) update after a change of evidence at a
> fraction of the cost of re-reading the task, (b) do so without losing decision quality relative
> to a model that reads the whole state jointly, and (c) keep improving from verified feedback
> without being retrained from scratch.

Not under test, and not claimed whatever the results: superiority over Jev or any hosted model
(none is called); benefit to real users (no human study is run); novelty of any single ingredient.

## 2. Systems

| Id | System | Reader | Combination | Recomputation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E** | EASE-Delta | Stage A edge model, one pair at a time | learned edge refinement + exact policy + exact logic | incremental |
| **E-full** | same model, forced rebuild | as E | as E | everything, every event |
| **B1-soft** | calibrated rules | Stage A edge model | temperature-calibrated probabilities + exact policy | incremental |
| **B1-hard** | classifier and if-statements | Stage A edge model | top label per edge + exact policy | incremental |
| **N** | neural aggregator (ablation) | Stage A edge model | GRU over ranked edges, policy learned | incremental |
| **B2** | dense reader | ModernBERT-base initialised from the Stage A weights, fine-tuned to read a claim against *all* active records concatenated | 4-way status head, then the same exact logic | every predicate, every changed event |
| **B3** | public NLI model in the same wrapper | `tasksource/ModernBERT-base-nli` | as B1-soft | incremental |

All systems share the ledger, the task schema, the exact logic and the planner. B2 is the decisive
baseline: same backbone, same pretraining, same downstream training episodes, same wrapper.
A duplicate or stale delivery leaves the ledger unchanged and therefore costs every system nothing.

## 3. Data

| Pool | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Stage A train | streamed train splits of VitaminC, MNLI, WANLI, plus synthetic unrelated pairs | edge model |
| dev | VitaminC validation[:6001], MNLI validation_matched[:5000], SNLI validation[:3000], unrelated.dev | monitoring, temperature |
| Stage B pool | VitaminC validation[6001:], MNLI validation_matched[5000:] | training and tuning everything downstream of the edge model, including B2; calibration of thresholds |
| test pool | VitaminC test, MNLI validation_mismatched, WANLI test | final evaluation only |
| external | STALE (400 scenarios), ANLI test r1-r3, SNLI test | final evaluation only; ANLI is CC BY-NC and is never trained on |

Leakage audit (`runs/leakage_audit.json`, exact string match): 0 of 68,752 Stage B pairs and 14 of
79,851 test-pool pairs occur in any training split. The 14 are left in place and reported.

SNLI is excluded from training and from episode ground truth because its labels assume premise
and hypothesis describe the same scene. A pilot model trained with SNLI read 11.8% of unrelated
pairs as decisive (`scratch/probe_unrelated.py`). This decision was made on dev data before this
plan was written.

Episodes are generated by `ease/data/episodes.py`. Generator settings:

* **STANDARD**: 3-8 predicates, 0-8 distractors at setup, 6-14 events. Used for training (from the
  Stage B pool) and for testing (from the test pool).
* **WIDE**: 9-16 predicates, 10-30 distractors at setup, 12-24 events, wider formulas. Test only.
  No component sees a WIDE episode before the final evaluation.

Test sets: 1,000 STANDARD episodes (generator seed 20260929) and 300 WIDE episodes (seed 20260930)
from the test pool. Setup steps are executed but not scored.

## 4. Metrics

Per (action, scored step):

* **disposition accuracy**: system disposition equals ground truth (READY / BLOCKED / NEEDS_INFO).
* **stale-decision rate**: among steps where the ground-truth disposition changed at that step,
  the fraction where the system's disposition after the event is not the new ground truth.
* **spurious-change rate**: among steps where ground truth did not change, the fraction where the
  system's disposition changed.
* **false-READY rate**: among steps where truth is not READY, the fraction the system calls READY.
  This is the costly error, since READY is what gets proposed to a person.

Per (predicate, scored step): 4-way status accuracy and negative log-likelihood.

Probability quality: Brier score and binary calibration error (15 bins) of predicted P(success)
against the simulator's sampled outcomes.

Cost, per event that changed the ledger: encoder tokens processed, pairs or contexts encoded,
wall-clock seconds (warm model, idle machine, p50 and p95).

Uncertainty: 95% percentile bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, resampling whole episodes. Differences
between systems are paired by episode.

## 5. Hypotheses and decision rules

**H1 Exactness.** With the scorer in canonical mode, after every event of 200 STANDARD test
episodes, every cached node equals an independent full rebuild bit for bit.
*Supported iff* zero nodes differ. The non-canonical maximum absolute difference is reported too.

**H2 Update cost.** On events that change the ledger, E processes at most half the encoder tokens
of E-full and of B2, and takes at most half the wall-clock time.
*Supported iff* the upper end of the 95% interval of each ratio is at most 0.5, on STANDARD and on
WIDE separately. Also reported, with the expectation that the advantage disappears: the cost of a
weights change and of a schema change that touches every predicate.

**H3 Revision fidelity.** On STANDARD, E's stale-decision rate is lower than B2's, and E's
disposition accuracy is not more than 1 percentage point below B2's.
*Supported iff* the 95% interval of (B2 stale rate - E stale rate) lies above 0 and the lower end
of the interval of (E accuracy - B2 accuracy) is above -0.01.

**H4 Unseen structure.** The same two comparisons on WIDE.

**H5 Learned refinement.** E has lower predicate-status NLL and not lower accuracy than B1-soft on
STANDARD and WIDE. *Supported iff* the interval of the NLL difference excludes 0 in E's favour on
both. If not supported, the conclusion is that calibrated rules suffice and the refiner is
unnecessary.

**H6 Policy learned versus executed.** N is compared with E on WIDE. Prediction: executing the
policy generalises better to unseen structure than learning it. Reported either way.

**H7 Self-evolution.** On a stream from a domain absent from training, with verified labels
returned for 30% of items:
(a) accuracy over the second half of the stream is higher with evolution than frozen, interval
    excluding 0;
(b) accuracy on the frozen regression suite (dev sets) falls by no more than 0.5 points after any
    promoted consolidation;
(c) when 50% of returned labels are wrong, no consolidation that lowers regression accuracy by
    more than 0.5 points is promoted.
*Supported iff* all three hold.

**H8 Dependency proposals.** With the threshold chosen by conformal risk control at alpha = 0.05
on Stage B episodes, the mean fraction of true links missed on test episodes is at most 0.05, and
edges scored per changed event on WIDE fall by at least a factor of 2 compared with linking every
record to every predicate. *Supported iff* both hold. The resulting change in disposition accuracy
is reported; a loss of more than 1 point means the proposer should stay off by default.

**H9 Implicit conflicts (exploratory).** On STALE, the edge model is asked whether the new
observation refutes the old one, at a threshold giving a 5% false-positive rate on non-conflicting
control pairs from the same scenarios. Prediction, stated in advance: detection of propagated
conflicts (type T2) is substantially lower than of direct ones (T1), because nothing in training
teaches multi-step consequence. These figures use STALE differently from its authors and are not
comparable with the accuracies in their paper.

## 6. What would refute the architecture

If H3 and H4 both fail, a dense reader inside the same wrapper decides at least as well, and the
remaining advantage is cost alone. If H2 also fails, there is no advantage. Either outcome is
reported as the result.

If H5 fails, the learned combination stage adds nothing over calibrated rules and should be
removed. If H8 fails, learned sparsity is unsafe at this size and every record should be compared
with every predicate.

## 7. Reported regardless of outcome

Every metric above for every system on both test sets; per-event-type breakdowns; worst cases
(episodes where E does worst); accuracy of the edge model on every evaluation set including ANLI
and SNLI where it is expected to be weak; the false-decisive rate on unrelated pairs; training
cost in examples, time and tokens; all failures encountered during development that changed the
design.
